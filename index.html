This isn't just a feeling. The research is clear.

  • Katterman et al., 2014 · Eating Behaviors

    A systematic review of 14 studies found that mindfulness meditation effectively reduced both binge eating and emotional eating, with participants consistently showing decreases in eating driven by emotion rather than hunger.

  • Kristeller & Wolever, 2014 · Mindfulness (Springer)

    In a randomized clinical trial of 150 participants, 95% of those in the mindfulness-based program no longer met criteria for binge eating disorder after four months. The amount of personal practice directly predicted improvement.

  • Dalen et al., 2010 · Complementary Therapies in Medicine

    Participants showed significant decreases in depression, perceived stress, and negative affect alongside improved eating behavior, suggesting mindful eating supports broader psychological wellbeing.

  • Jordan et al., 2014 · Personality and Individual Differences

    Even brief periods of mindful awareness led to less impulsive eating, lower calorie consumption, and healthier food choices, shifting participants toward more intentional, less reactive eating patterns.
